## Project Overview
An independent dashboard concept for studying a fictional Hajj-service operations scenario, including visitor flows, capacity utilization, service requests, and operational KPIs.

## Business Problem
Large service operations involve multiple locations, schedules, teams, and service demands. Without consolidated reporting, simulated resource bottlenecks and service patterns may be difficult to interpret.

## Proposed Solution
Create a Power BI dashboard using clearly labeled synthetic operational records to explore demand over time, service workload, capacity, and hypothetical response patterns.

## Objectives
- Model fictional service locations, time periods, activities, and demand.
- Explore occupancy, arrivals, and service request patterns.
- Present time-based and location-based KPIs through interactive visualizations.
- Demonstrate a reproducible pipeline for preparation and reporting.

---

## Dataset Description
Entirely synthetic Hajj-related service-operations scenarios; no real pilgrim identity, confidential operator data, or official live monitoring claims.

## Methodology
1. Define a fictional operations scenario, metric glossary, and data entities.
2. Generate synthetic time-series and service-operation records.
3. Clean, validate, and aggregate the dataset.
4. Build Power BI relationships, calculations, and interactive charts.
5. Document interpretations with synthetic-data limitations.

## Exploratory Data Analysis
Planned exploration includes simulated hourly demand, resource capacity, service volume, and possible operational peaks.

## Data Modeling
A time, location, service-type, and activity-based reporting model is planned; capacity definitions and denominator consistency will be documented.
